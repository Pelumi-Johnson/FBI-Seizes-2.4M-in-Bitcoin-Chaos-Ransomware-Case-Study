This presentation examines the FBI’s seizure of $2.4 million in Bitcoin linked to the Chaos ransomware operation, highlighting how ransomware groups operate, how cryptocurrency can be traced through blockchain analysis, and how law enforcement disrupts cybercriminal networks.

## 🔍 Key Focus Areas
- Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) operational models  
- Bitcoin tracing and blockchain forensic analysis  
- Law enforcement wallet seizures and civil forfeiture  
- Connections between Chaos ransomware affiliates and related groups  
- Broader implications for cybersecurity defense and cybercrime prosecution
